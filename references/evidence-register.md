## Claims deliberately excluded

- "AI Overviews appear in 45% / 60% of searches." Figures range from 13% to 60%+ depending on study, query mix, and date. Cite a specific study with its date if the number is needed.
- "Schema increases AI visibility 30–40%." Vendor claim without a public methodology; contradicted by Google's guidance and one published no-correlation finding.
- "Perplexity prefers FAQ schema and PDFs." Reported in playbooks; no vendor confirmation. Retained in matrices as "reported; unconfirmed."
